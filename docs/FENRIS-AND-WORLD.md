# Fenris and his world — lore handoff

This is a curated development reference for **Book of Fenris / Fenris Awakening**, not a new story chapter or a complete lore archive. It should be sufficient to orient another session without access to the author's local Valora folder. Source keys below identify the material used; the original files are not required to read this brief.

## Authority and knowledge boundaries

The user authors the story. Their explicit instructions for this game take precedence over every older manuscript, biography, or file labelled CANON. The archive contains revisions, duplicated material, different eras, and incompatible character versions. Do not combine them into an invented explanation.

Keep three things separate: what the developer knows from later lore, what Fenris knows at the opening, and what the author has approved for implementation. Background in this document does not authorize new scenes, companions, quests, dialogue, powers, or revelations.

## The opening: confirmed for this game [A]

- Approximately **1468 DR**, before Valora exists and before Fenris meets Croft.
- Fenris is a **large Lupera**. He is alone.
- He has **no knowledge of who he is or where he came from**. The interface may name him for the player; that does not establish that he knows his name or history.
- He begins with **no weapons or magical abilities**. Do not reinterpret this as merely having forgotten how to use existing powers. Shaman is the technical class direction, but he currently has no active class or class powers. Starting stats are STR 14, DEX 14, CON 16, INT 14, WIS 16, CHA 14, AC 12, HP 13, and one d10 hit die.
- **The voices wake Fenris. His first objective is to seek their source**, a search lasting a couple of days, potentially including visits to a nearby village. The spirits are not found in the current opening. Nearby observations do not complete this objective.
- **Village and combat direction, confirmed 26 October 2026:** the nearby village has no guards. NPCs use level-1 human stats, with tougher prominent residents such as the blacksmith. Every world character needs stats because anyone may be involved in combat. The future combat system is turn-based; combat itself is not implemented yet.
- **Author-only knowledge, confirmed 25 October 2026:** the voices belong to spirits. This must not be revealed in opening dialogue, UI, journal entries, or interactions. Fenris does not yet know. The specific spirits, eventual encounter, cause of his amnesia, his origin, and exact location remain unspecified.

The forest now generates as Fenris explores, with varied groves, undergrowth, clearings, stones and streams. Water is traversable at 30% movement speed; there is no bridge. Interactable objects use faint glowing baubles instead of floating labels. The starting clearing, campfire, observation text, and current voice lines remain prototype choices. They are not automatically fixed geography or authored plot. Hearing a voice does not establish telepathy, spirit magic, or a supernatural bond. No companion, combat encounter, vision quest, or elemental trial is approved by this brief.

## What a Lupera is [B, C]

Lupera are fully sapient, bipedal wolf-humanoids: people capable of speech, judgment, culture, and dexterous tool use. They are not ordinary wolves, feral monsters, or automatically werewolves. Do not add a transformed-human or lycanthropic origin to Fenris.

Their physical vocabulary includes a fully furred body, pronounced muzzle, upright expressive ears, digitigrade legs, clawed hands with full dexterity, and a tail used for balance and expression. Their smell and hearing are strong; the species reference also describes good low-light vision. These are descriptive traits, not an approved package of gameplay abilities or numerical bonuses.

**Visual direction updated October 2026:** the author chose 2D pixel art for Fenris. The current opening uses the approved white-and-grey Fenris with brown shorts, an upright build, and custom idle, walking, sleeping, sitting, and waking animations. Fenris remains a Lupera; no transformation backstory is established.

The earlier 3D visual reference depicted him as tall and heavily built, muscular, with grey-white fur, pale blue eyes, a natural wolf muzzle, expressive pointed ears, clawed hands and paws, a bare torso, and worn brown shorts tied with rope. This description and the procedural model are historical visual references. The reference image itself is not included in the repository.

Later biographies include exact measurements, ages, scars, ceremonial clothing, and weapons. Those details are not all confirmed for the amnesiac opening. In particular, do not equip the later battleaxe Gorehowl, armour, totems, royal insignia, or glowing magical marks. [A, C, D]

## Understanding Fenris without assigning his choices [C, D]

Later character writing consistently presents a physically imposing person whose strength is tempered by empathy and restraint. Protection matters more to him than displaying dominance. Force is available to the later Fenris, but is not his answer to every problem. He can be curious, blunt, dryly funny, reactive, and surprisingly gentle; he is not simply a grim, growling brute.

His attention and emotions can be readable through ears, gaze, posture, shoulders, breathing, and tail. Plain speech and meaningful physical reactions suit him better than constant grand speeches or exaggerated animal mannerisms. His intelligence must not be reduced to a wolf stereotype.

These are characterization references drawn from later eras, **not a complete prescribed personality for the first scene**. They do not give the newly awakened Fenris remembered kingship, experienced leadership, known relationships, or conscious awareness of future power. The author determines how much continuity in temperament survives the amnesia. Player choices and author-written intent determine his actions.

## The world around the story [B, E, F, H]

The archive places this story in a **Dungeons & Dragons / Forgotten Realms-compatible world**, including Faerûn, with the author's own peoples, history, and political developments. DR means Dale Reckoning. External setting references should not silently overwrite the author's version of events.

The world contains humanlike peoples and visibly nonhuman peoples, including standard fantasy peoples and the author's animal-rooted peoples. Examples include Lupera (wolf-rooted) and Felari (cat-rooted). Appearance is not a substitute for intelligence or personhood. Exclusion, fear of difference, refuge, protection, and belonging are important background concerns.

**Terminology matters:** the archive uses *Kin* for conventionally humanlike peoples such as humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, and gnomes. It does not mean Lupera or animal-rooted peoples. The later founding history includes racist Kin opposition and their expulsion. This is specific political history; do not confuse it with a generic label for beastfolk, move the expulsion into 1468, or invent an encounter from it.

Magic is an ordinary part of the setting, including society, travel, conflict, religion, and work. The setting's magic reference distinguishes magical methods from mundane ones and leaves individual capability to individual character canon. **A magical world does not grant opening-era Fenris magic.** His natural strength, senses, and movement should not acquire supernatural explanations by default.

### Geography: regional context, not an opening location [F, G]

The later kingdom of Valora lies on the northern coast of the Sea of Stars, east of Cormyr and west of Sembia. Its geographic reference describes forest-heavy northern areas, an eastern trade corridor, and a strategically important southern coastline. The later kingdom includes places such as Daerlun, Mulhessen, Urmlaspyr, and Verdance Point.

That geography describes a developed kingdom, particularly the 1489-era map. It must not be used as a 1468 political map. The awakening clearing has not been located within those borders, and no named grove, road, settlement, or regional faction has been assigned to it by the author.

Croft is a male sabre-tooth tiger who becomes Fenris's companion: golden fur with black stripes, amber eyes, intelligent and loyal. His biography says he understands Common but does not speak it, communicating through body language, sounds, and his bond with Fenris. Later armour and the established bond are not starting equipment or pre-existing opening facts. [I]

Valora's later identity is relevant because this is the story before that achievement: a wandering outsider eventually becomes a protector and an elected leader. The kingdom grows through conflict, reform, refuge, and practical institutions, rather than beginning as an ancient inherited monarchy. That is thematic context, not permission to predetermine the player's choices. [E, G]

## Legacy material and unresolved questions

The supplied **01-The Story Begins.txt** provides a forest/campfire atmosphere and the line “Do you hear them calling?” It also contains an orc version of Fenris, known ancestry (“son of Qairne”), weapons, a totem, spellcasting, a rot-spirit confrontation, and a vision-quest explanation. Those incompatible details are **not current opening canon**. Do not solve the discrepancy by adding a transformation, hidden inventory, or remembered past. The corrupted grove and elemental journey are source material for the author to revise, not approved opening quests. [J, A]

Older timelines also use “Valora Year 1” rather than DR. The current civic timeline explicitly converts those years with **Valora year + 1470 = DR**, placing the founding in 1471 DR. Do not mistake a later file's “present” for this game's opening era. [E]

Still for the author to decide: the exact awakening location and circumstances; whether/how Fenris learns his name; the identities of the spirits and when their nature is revealed; how his past is revealed; the first encounter after waking; when Croft appears; and how future abilities, equipment, relationships, and companions become part of the story. No answers are invented here.
